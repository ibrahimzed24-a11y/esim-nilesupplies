Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an eSIM and how does it work for international travel?
An eSIM is a digital microchip built into modern smartphones that allows you to activate cellular data packages without physical SIM cards. You purchase online, scan a QR code, and connect immediately upon landing in Asia.
Do I have to remove my home physical SIM card?
No. You can leave your home SIM active for incoming phone calls and bank SMS two-factor authentication, while designating the travel eSIM exclusively for mobile cellular data roaming.
How does an eSIM bypass Indonesia's airport IMEI customs tax in Bali?
Indonesia's customs (Bea Cukai) IMEI registration rule applies strictly to local physical SIM cards. Travel eSIMs utilize international roaming agreements on Telkomsel, which are completely exempt from IMEI registration and tax declarations.
Can I tether my laptop via Personal Hotspot with these eSIMs?
Yes. Airalo, Saily, and Yesim all permit unrestricted personal hotspot sharing across laptops, tablets, and secondary devices.
