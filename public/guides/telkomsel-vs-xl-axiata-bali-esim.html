Telkomsel vs XL Axiata: Bali Tourist eSIM Network Battle

📍 Indonesia (Bali) ⏱️ 7 min read 📡 Network: Telkomsel vs XL Axiata 🛡️ Fact-Checked: 2026 Edition

When picking a Bali eSIM, the underlying local roaming carrier makes all the difference. We pitted Indonesia's two network titans, Telkomsel and XL Axiata, in a comprehensive island-wide speed and reliability shootout.

Network Architecture & Market Share

Telkomsel (majority-owned by the Indonesian state and Singtel) is the undisputed heavyweight with over 160 million subscribers and over 250,000 base transceiver stations across the archipelago. XL Axiata (Axiata Group) is a formidable competitor with strong urban penetration in Java and southern Bali.

Head-to-Head Testing Across 5 Bali Regions

Our field tests logged download and upload performance across five key tourist zones: 1. **Seminyak / Kuta:** Telkomsel 115 Mbps vs. XL 88 Mbps. 2. **Canggu Echo Beach:** Telkomsel 92 Mbps vs. XL 74 Mbps. 3. **Ubud Monkey Forest:** Telkomsel 68 Mbps vs. XL 32 Mbps. 4. **Uluwatu Cliffs:** Telkomsel 54 Mbps vs. XL 21 Mbps. 5. **Nusa Penida Harbor:** Telkomsel 45 Mbps vs. XL 12 Mbps (frequent drops).

Telkomsel consistently delivered lower jitter, higher throughput, and broader rural reach.

Which eSIM Uses Which Carrier?

Airalo (Indotel) exclusively roams on Telkomsel. Saily connects primarily to Telkomsel and Indosat. Yesim offers dynamic network selection between available partner networks.

Frequently Asked Questions: Indonesia (Bali) eSIMs

Q: Is Telkomsel faster than XL in Bali?
Yes. Across both urban beach clubs and rural inland hills, Telkomsel delivers 30-60% faster average download speeds.
Q: Can I manually select my network on an eSIM in Bali?
Yes, under Cellular Settings > Network Selection, disable 'Automatic' to choose Telkomsel directly.
Q: Does XL Axiata have 5G in Bali?
XL has limited 5G pockets in Denpasar and Kuta; Telkomsel's 5G rollout is significantly more widespread.
Q: Which Bali eSIM has the lowest latency for gaming and calls?
Airalo Indotel on Telkomsel logs the lowest ping times (28-40ms to regional Singapore servers).
