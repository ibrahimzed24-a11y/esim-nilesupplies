How to Fix Slow eSIM Data & Roaming Drops in Asia: Troubleshooting Guide

📍 Troubleshooting ⏱️ 8 min read 📡 Network: Universal Diagnostics 🛡️ Fact-Checked: 2026 Edition

Landing abroad and finding your phone showing '3G' or 'Edge' speeds can ruin a vacation morning. Before contacting customer support, follow these 6 proven field fixes.

Fix 1: Toggle Airplane Mode & Power Cycle

When transitioning between international roaming partners, phone baseband modems can get stuck on deprecated 3G signaling channels. Toggling Airplane Mode ON for 15 seconds forces the radio to re-negotiate with the nearest 5G/LTE tower.

Fix 2: Disable Automatic Network Selection

By default, phones choose networks automatically, sometimes selecting weak roaming partners. Go to Cellular > Network Selection, disable 'Automatic', and wait for available networks to populate. Manually tap the premier carrier (e.g., Telkomsel in Bali, Ooredoo in Maldives, Singtel in Singapore).

Fix 3: Verify APN & Data Roaming Toggle

Two critical settings account for 90% of connectivity failures: 1. **Data Roaming:** Must be switched ON for the travel eSIM line. 2. **APN Name:** Check that your APN matches your provider instructions (e.g., 'airalo' or 'saily').

Fix 4: Reset Network Settings (The Nuclear Option)

If persistent connection drops occur, go to Settings > General > Transfer or Reset > Reset > Reset Network Settings. This clears corrupted carrier caches without deleting personal photos or apps.

Frequently Asked Questions: Troubleshooting eSIMs

Q: Why is my 5G phone only showing LTE or 4G in Asia?
Not all roaming partner agreements grant immediate 5G access, or 5G may be restricted to urban centers. High-speed LTE is often equally fast.
Q: Will resetting network settings delete my eSIM?
No. Resetting network settings deletes saved Wi-Fi passwords and Bluetooth pairings, but leaves eSIM profiles untouched.
Q: Why does my data work for Google Maps but not Instagram?
Check if 'Cellular Data' permission is disabled for specific apps under your phone's cellular settings.
Q: How fast do eSIM support teams respond?
Airalo, Saily, and Yesim all offer 24/7 in-app live chat support with response times under 15 minutes.
