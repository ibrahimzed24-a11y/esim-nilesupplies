Affiliate & Commercial Disclosure

Compliance: FTC 16 CFR § 255.5 Operator: Nile Supplies (contact@nilesupplies.com) Last Updated: 2026

1. Full FTC Compliance Statement

The United States Federal Trade Commission (FTC) requires digital publications to transparently disclose any commercial relationships, sponsorships, endorsements, or financial incentives that may influence recommendations.

NileRoam (hosted at https://e-sim.nilesupplies.com and operated by Nile Supplies, contact@nilesupplies.com) participates in multiple travel affiliate marketing programs. This means that when you click on certain buttons, links, banners, or promo codes on this website and subsequently complete a purchase or booking, Nile Supplies may earn a monetary commission or referral fee.

Crucially, this occurs at absolutely zero extra cost to you. In many instances, using our affiliate links or exclusive coupon codes (such as NEWTOAIRALO15, NILEROAM10, or YESIM15) provides you with a discounted rate below the standard retail price.

3. Technical Implementation of Sponsored Links

In strict compliance with Google Webmaster Guidelines and international search engine standards:

  • All commercial outbound links automatically carry the HTML attributes rel="nofollow sponsored noopener".
  • All affiliate click routes (including /api/go) send HTTP 307 temporary redirects with X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow headers to prevent artificial search index manipulation.

4. Editorial Independence & Objectivity

Affiliate commissions fund our research, server hosting, and rigorous network testing. However, financial incentives never dictate our editorial verdict:

  • We actively publish latency shortcomings, throttle thresholds, and carrier coverage blind spots across islands and rural atolls.
  • When a physical local SIM purchased at an airport is objectively cheaper or offers faster local voice calling than an eSIM, we state so openly in our guides.
  • Our Trip Data Calculator algorithm estimates bandwidth strictly based on technical data requirements, not affiliate payout rates.
