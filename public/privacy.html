Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 Operator: Nile Supplies (contact@nilesupplies.com)

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Nile Supplies ("we", "us", or "our"), operating the website NileRoam at https://e-sim.nilesupplies.com and https://nilesupplies.com, collects, uses, and safeguards information when you visit our website, utilize our interactive comparison tools, or follow affiliate links to third-party providers.

2. Information We Collect

We respect your privacy and collect minimal data necessary to deliver a fast and useful experience:

  • Log & Usage Data: When you access our website, our web servers and content delivery network (Vercel) automatically log technical request data including your browser type, operating system, referring URL, IP address (anonymized where required by law), and pages viewed.
  • Interactive Tool Inputs: Usage of the Trip Data Calculator or Device Compatibility Search runs entirely on the client side in your browser. We do not store or transmit your private calculations or device selections to external databases.
  • Direct Communications: If you contact us via email at contact@nilesupplies.com, we retain your email address and message contents solely to respond to your inquiry.

3. Cookies and Affiliate Tracking

Our website does not use tracking cookies for intrusive advertising. However, when you click an external link to an eSIM or travel partner (such as Airalo, Saily, Yesim, Klook, SafetyWing, or Skyscanner), you are directed to their platform via affiliate network parameters (such as Travelpayouts or Impact.com).

These third-party platforms use cookies and tracking identifiers to credit Nile Supplies with a referral commission if you complete an eligible purchase. These cookies are governed by the respective third-party privacy policies:

  • Impact.com: Privacy Policy available at impact.com/privacy-policy
  • Travelpayouts: Privacy Policy available at travelpayouts.com/privacy
  • Airalo: Privacy Policy available at airalo.com/more-info/privacy-policy
  • Saily: Privacy Policy available at saily.com/privacy-policy
  • Yesim: Privacy Policy available at yesim.app/privacy-policy

4. Data Retention & Security

We employ industry-standard HTTPS encryption (TLS 1.3) across our entire domain. We do not store financial information, credit card numbers, or passport records on our servers; all checkout transactions occur directly on the encrypted payment gateways of our verified merchant partners.

5. Your Rights Under GDPR & CCPA

Depending on your residency, you may have rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to request access, correction, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you. Because we do not require account registration or store personal customer profiles, we hold virtually zero identifiable data. To exercise any data privacy request, contact us at contact@nilesupplies.com.
