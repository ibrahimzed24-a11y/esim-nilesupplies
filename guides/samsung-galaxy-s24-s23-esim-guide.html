Samsung Galaxy S24 / S23 Travel eSIM Setup: SIM Manager & APN Tips

📍 Samsung One UI ⏱️ 7 min read 📡 Network: Samsung Android 🛡️ Fact-Checked: 2026 Edition

Samsung Galaxy flagships feature one of the most powerful and versatile SIM Managers in the Android ecosystem. Here is how to configure your Galaxy S24 or S23 for flawless roaming in Asia.

Navigating Samsung SIM Manager

On your Galaxy device, open Settings > Connections > SIM Manager. Tap 'Add Mobile Plan' > 'Scan QR Code from Service Provider'. Hold your camera over the QR code sent by your provider and tap 'Add'.

Preferred SIM Card Configuration

Under 'Preferred SIM Card': - **Calls:** Set to Primary (Home SIM). - **Messages:** Set to Primary (Home SIM). - **Mobile Data:** Set to Travel eSIM. - **Auto Data Switching:** Toggle OFF to prevent accidental home data roaming fees.

Samsung APN Configuration Checklist

Most travel eSIMs configure Access Point Names (APN) automatically. If data fails to flow upon landing, go to Connections > Mobile Networks > Access Point Names > Add. Enter the APN specified in your provider's setup email (e.g., 'globaldata' or 'airalo') and tap Save.

Frequently Asked Questions: Samsung One UI eSIMs

Q: Do all Samsung Galaxy S24 models support eSIM?
Yes, all global Galaxy S24, S23, and S22 models support eSIM (except select dual-physical SIM models in mainland China).
Q: Can I toggle my travel eSIM off when using hotel Wi-Fi?
Yes, in SIM Manager you can toggle the eSIM off to conserve power, but leaving it on does not consume data while Wi-Fi is active.
Q: How many eSIMs can I store on a Samsung phone?
You can store between 5 and 7 profiles, with 1 active alongside a physical SIM.
Q: Does Samsung Smart Switch transfer eSIMs to a new phone?
Travel eSIMs are single-use; do not delete the profile, as QR codes cannot typically be re-scanned on another device.
